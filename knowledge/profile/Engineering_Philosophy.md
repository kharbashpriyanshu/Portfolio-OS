# Engineering Philosophy

## 1. Security by Design

I believe that security is not an afterthought or a bolt-on feature, but a fundamental architectural requirement. Whether building a full-stack application or an automated reconnaissance tool, zero-trust principles and explicit boundary enforcement are integrated from day one.

## 2. Evidence-Based Threat Detection

Effective detection engineering relies on structured data, intelligent analysis, and minimizing alert fatigue. By utilizing clear APIs (like Gemini for unstructured log analysis), I focus on reducing MTTR (Mean Time To Respond) and isolating actionable intelligence from background noise.

## 3. The Power of Automation

Manual repetitive tasks are the enemy of scale and security. I leverage Python, automation pipelines, and robust testing frameworks (e.g., Pytest with 100% test coverage targets) to automate vulnerability scanning, asset discovery, and regression testing.

## 4. Continuous Iteration & Learning

Cybersecurity evolves rapidly. My approach to engineering requires continuous hands-on labs (like TryHackMe), analyzing modern attack vectors in isolated environments, and earning industry-recognized certifications to maintain technical excellence.
